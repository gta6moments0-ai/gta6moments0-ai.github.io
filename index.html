What we do

GTA 6 Moments turns long GTA 6 livestreams into short vertical clips: the funniest, wildest, and most surprising seconds, edited with captions and posted daily on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Every clip credits the streamer whose gameplay it shows. We only clip gameplay from streamers whose content we are allowed to use, and we never pass off anyone else's footage as our own.

How posting works

Our TikTok app posts clips to @spyd3rgaming1 using TikTok's official Content Posting API. It only reads the account info it needs to post and to see how clips perform — nothing else.
