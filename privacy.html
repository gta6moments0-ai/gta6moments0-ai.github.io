Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What this website collects

Nothing. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and collects no personal data from visitors. It is a static information page.

2. What our TikTok app accesses

Our TikTok app connects only to our own TikTok account (@spyd3rgaming1) using TikTok's official API, with these permissions:

  • user.info.basic — basic account information, to confirm which account the app is posting to.
  • user.info.stats — account statistics, to see how our clips perform.
  • video.list — a list of our own posted videos and their performance.
  • video.upload and video.publish — to upload and publish our own edited clips.

The app uses these permissions only to post our clips and read our own performance data. It does not access other users' accounts, messages, or private data, and it does not sell or share data with advertisers.
